## Address plan

<Lede>
  Custom mode, so every range is a decision. Nothing overlaps, because peering
  and VPN both refuse overlapping ranges and you cannot fix it later without
  renumbering.
</Lede>

### Why it is laid out this way

<Folds>
  <Fold
    name="ip-plan"
    mark="1"
    title="A whole /16 per region, carved into much smaller subnets"
  >
    Subnets can be expanded but never shrunk or moved. Leaving room inside the
    region block means the next subnet never has to go somewhere awkward.
  </Fold>
  <Fold name="ip-plan" mark="2" title="A /16 reserved for PSA, not a /24">
    Every managed service with a private IP draws from this pool. Running out
    means a disruptive re-allocation, and the reservation costs nothing while
    unused.
  </Fold>
  <Fold
    name="ip-plan"
    mark="3"
    title="The proxy-only subnet at the far end of the region block"
  >
    It is a whole subnet that holds none of your resources. Putting it at
    `10.10.250.0/24` keeps it clear of the ranges you will actually grow into.
  </Fold>
</Folds>
