## The component chain

<Lede>
  A proxy load balancer is not one object. Walk the chain link by link, or click
  any link, to see what lives there. The two attachment points worth memorising
  are the target proxy, which holds everything about TLS, and the backend
  service, which holds everything about health, balancing, the WAF, and the CDN.
</Lede>
