## The story

Kadé is a small online grocery shop in Colombo. Customers order from a mobile app and a website. Both call one backend at `api.kade.lk`.

You are Kadé's backend engineer. You work from home on your laptop, on your home WiFi. The backend runs in Google Cloud (GCP): one VM for the API and one VM for the database.

Kadé starts simple on purpose. As the course goes on, we will hit real problems with this setup and fix them, adding the pieces real systems use.
