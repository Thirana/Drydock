### Three things this plan is deliberately doing

| Choice                                                     | Reason                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A whole `/16` per region, carved into much smaller subnets | Subnets can be expanded but never shrunk or moved. Leaving room inside the region block means the next subnet never has to go somewhere awkward.            |
| A `/16` reserved for PSA, not a `/24`                      | Every managed service with a private IP draws from this pool. Running out means a disruptive re-allocation, and the reservation costs nothing while unused. |
| Proxy-only subnet at the far end of the region block       | It is a whole subnet that holds none of your resources. Putting it at `10.10.250.0/24` keeps it clear of the ranges you will actually grow into.            |

<Note>
  A `/20` gives 4,091 usable addresses, not 4,094. GCP reserves four in every
  subnet: network, default gateway, second-to-last, and broadcast. It matters on
  small subnets, not on this one.
</Note>
